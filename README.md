# Model-Cross-Validation
I found a study titled, In vivo antiviral host response to SARS-CoV-2 by viral load, sex, and age [dataset I]: https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi. This study inspected “host gene expression across infection status, viral load, age, and sex among RNA-sequencing profiles”. It had RNA-seq results from 430 individuals with SARS-CoV-2 and a negative control group of 54. The results from the study suggest “strong interferon-driven antiviral response and reduced transcription of ribosomal proteins.” Indeed, there were specific genes that did correlate with viral load which are explored in the Analysis section.
